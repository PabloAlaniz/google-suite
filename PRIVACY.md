# Privacy policy

The gsuite-sdk plugin (the skill and the gsuite-mcp server) collects no data.

- It runs entirely on your machine and talks only to Google's APIs, on your
  behalf, with your own OAuth client and token.
- Your OAuth token stays in a local file (`tokens.db`) and is never sent
  anywhere except to Google.
- Nothing you read or write through it (emails, events, files, sheets, tasks,
  contacts) is sent to the plugin author or any third party.
- There are no analytics, telemetry, tracking or ads, and the author has no
  servers that receive data.

Google's handling of your data is covered by Google's own privacy policy.
Content that Claude reads through the plugin becomes part of your Claude
conversation, covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/PabloAlaniz/google-suite/issues.
